FALL is a mining roguelite. No account or login is required. Single-player works fully offline.

How to play: drag the on-screen joystick to move; the miner digs in the direction of movement and fires automatically at nearby enemies. Tap ATÖLYE (Workshop) at the surface camp to buy upgrades with collected ore.

Optional co-op ("Birlikte Kaz" / Dig Together) in the main menu: one player taps ODA KUR (Create Room) and shares the 4-letter room code; the other enters it under ODAYA KATIL (Join Room). HIZLI EŞLEŞ (Quick Match) pairs two waiting players automatically. Testing co-op needs two devices; single-player is fully testable on one device. The connection is peer-to-peer (WebRTC) and needs internet only while playing co-op. No personal data is exchanged or stored.

The app collects no data, contains no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The in-game language is Turkish.
